The quality of olive oil also depends on proper storage. Our tin container is not just about appearance; it is carefully chosen to help protect the oil and preserve its quality.

1. 100% protection from light:
Light, especially ultraviolet light, can accelerate olive oil oxidation and affect its quality and flavor. The tin container provides strong protection from light.

2. Protection and safety:
Food-grade tin packaging helps protect the oil from external factors and preserve its purity and quality during storage.

3. Preserving the original flavor:
Protection from light and air helps the oil maintain its aromatic compounds and distinctive flavor for a longer period.

4. Durable and safe for transportation:
Tin containers are strong and durable, making them practical for transportation and storage while helping the product arrive safely.

To choose high-quality olive oil and make sure you are buying an authentic product, there are several important things to look for.

1. Look for low acidity:
Extra Virgin Olive Oil must meet specific quality standards. Acidity is one of the important indicators, and lower acidity, together with other quality standards, can be a positive sign.

2. Pay attention to the extraction method:
The way olives are processed and the way the oil is extracted can affect its quality and natural compounds. Choosing a trusted and clearly sourced product is important.

3. Check the taste and aroma:
Good olive oil usually has a distinctive aroma and flavor associated with fresh olives. A slight bitterness or peppery sensation can be a natural characteristic of olive oil.

4. Check the packaging and storage:
Olive oil is sensitive to light and heat. Dark or tin packaging can help protect it from light, while proper storage away from heat is also important.

1. الاستخدام الغذائي:
زيت الزيتون البكر الممتاز مناسب للاستخدام اليومي في الأكل، مثل السلطات والجبنة والأطباق المختلفة، ويمكن استخدامه في طرق طهي مناسبة حسب درجة الحرارة.

2. استخدامه في العناية بالشعر:
ممكن استخدام زيت الزيتون كجزء من روتين العناية بالشعر، خصوصًا لترطيب الشعر الجاف. جربي كمية صغيرة أولًا وتجنبي استخدامه إذا سبب لكِ تهيجًا.

3. استخدامه في العناية بالبشرة:
زيت الزيتون ممكن يستخدم كمرطب لبعض أنواع البشرة، لكن الأفضل تجربة كمية صغيرة على جزء محدود من الجلد أولًا للتأكد من عدم حدوث تهيج.

4. التدليك:
يمكن استخدام كمية مناسبة من زيت الزيتون للتدليك الخارجي، مع تجنب استخدامه على الجلد المتهيج أو المصاب.

1. Food use:
Extra Virgin Olive Oil is suitable for everyday cooking and meals, including salads, cheese, and many different dishes. It can also be used for suitable cooking methods depending on the temperature.

2. Hair care:
Olive oil can be used as part of a hair-care routine, especially for moisturizing dry hair. Start with a small amount and stop using it if it causes irritation.

3. Skin care:
Olive oil can work as a moisturizer for some skin types. It is best to test a small amount on a limited area first to make sure it does not cause irritation.

4. Massage:
A suitable amount of olive oil can be used for external massage. Avoid applying it to irritated or damaged skin.
