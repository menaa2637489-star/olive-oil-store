Olive oil quality depends not only on production but also on packaging and storage. Here are some advantages of using a suitable tin container:

1. Protection from light:
Light can accelerate olive oil oxidation and affect its quality and flavor. An opaque tin container helps protect the oil from light.

2. Packaging designed for olive oil:
Food-grade packaging suitable for contact with food helps preserve oil quality during storage, provided the container's internal lining is safe and intact.

3. Preserving flavor and quality:
Protection from light and limiting exposure to air after opening help preserve the oil's flavor and quality for longer.

4. Convenient transportation and storage:
Tin containers are durable and less likely to break during transportation than glass containers, helping the product arrive safely.

To choose high-quality olive oil, there are several important points that can help you make an informed decision.

1. Check the grade and acidity:
Extra Virgin Olive Oil must meet specific chemical and sensory standards, including a free acidity level not exceeding 0.8%. Lower acidity is an important indicator, but it is not the only proof of authenticity or quality.

2. Check the source and extraction method:
Choosing a product from a trusted source, with clear information about the olive variety, extraction method, and packaging, helps you assess its quality.

3. Pay attention to taste and aroma:
Extra Virgin Olive Oil has sensory characteristics associated with olives. It may have a degree of bitterness or a slight peppery sensation, but tasting alone cannot confirm authenticity.

4. Choose suitable packaging:
Olive oil is affected by light, heat, and air. Opaque packaging and suitable food-grade tin containers help protect it. Store the oil in a relatively cool place away from light.

With Abu El Nasr, pay attention to oil quality, source, and storage, and check the product label to make an informed choice.

Extra Virgin Olive Oil can be part of your daily routine in several ways. Here are some common uses:

1. For salads and everyday meals:
Add it to salads, cheese, vegetables, and different dishes to enjoy its authentic flavor and unsaturated fats as part of a balanced diet.

2. For cooking:
Olive oil can be used for suitable cooking methods. Pay attention to the cooking temperature and avoid heating the oil until it starts to smoke. The best choice depends on the recipe and required temperature.

3. As part of a balanced daily diet:
Use an appropriate amount as part of varied, balanced meals. Olive oil can contribute to a healthy dietary pattern, but it is not a substitute for medication or medical treatment.

4. Proper storage after use:
Close the container tightly after each use and store it away from sunlight and heat to help preserve the oil's quality and flavor.
