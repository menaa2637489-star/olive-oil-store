The liver is responsible for absorbing four essential vitamins.

Vitamin A:
For vision.

Vitamin K:
For kidney stones, gallstones, and atherosclerosis.

Vitamin E:
To prevent rancidity.

Vitamin D:
Fast absorption.

For the first time, olive oil has been enriched specifically with Vitamin D and Vitamin E to ensure 100% absorption and full benefit for the body.

للشرب والسلطات:

For drinking and salads:
To benefit fully from vitamins that support the liver and arteries.

For external application:
Studies have shown that applying olive oil enriched with Vitamin D to the joints strengthens bones and muscles and significantly relieves joint pain.

فيتامين D:
ضمان امتصاص 100%، وتقوية العظام والعضلات عند الدهان الموضعي للمفاصل.

الفيتامين الجميل لإنهاء تزنخ دهون الكبد وأكسدتها، وتقوية الكبد وحماية الجلد لصحة وعافية دائمة.

Vitamin D:

The wonderful vitamin for eliminating rancidity and oxidation of liver fats, strengthening the liver, and protecting the skin for lasting health and wellness.
